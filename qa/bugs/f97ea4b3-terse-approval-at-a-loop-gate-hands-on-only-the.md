# A terse approval at a LOOP gate hands on only the approval, never the work it approved

## Symptom

On a line with a review step (researcher > reviewer > LOOP until approved), a reviewer that approved without restating the work ("Looks good.\nVERDICT: approved") handed on nothing but "Looks good.". When the gate's DONE lane ran to the OUTBOX, "Looks good." was the line's whole result: the channel reply, the routine result, the job record and SEND TO all carried it instead of the research (in v0.13.5 too). When the DONE lane ran to another dock, that stage (a designer) was handed the approval and no research, because hops no longer replay the line's transcript (trunk only).

## Repro

1. Build INBOX > writer > reviewer > LOOP (until approved, DONE to the OUTBOX). The writer answers with three research findings; the reviewer answers "Looks good." then "VERDICT: approved".
2. RUN ONE REAL JOB (or STEP THROUGH).

## Verdict

Fixed in source on agent/terse-approval-fix (6471f0ade, with 85a881356 for the reworded-approval case, e0c1d62fd for a version the reviewer changed, 000e5680a for one of any length, 77b627c18 for a short version or a caveat, 524522d2c for an exhausted gate's frame and a self-loop's brief, and c7bce4337 for a frame inside a joined crate and a reply that claims a change). A bare approval (only the VERDICT line, or nothing but approval words such as "Looks good.", with no claim of a change) hands on the work it approved: to a dock framed with the approval as its review, out of the line the work alone, unclipped. Any other reply stays the work, as on trunk: a restatement, the reviewer's own version at any length (corrected, redacted, cut, tightened, reworded or translated), a claim that it changed the work ("Done."), a change it asks for, a withholding, or a review in its own words. A crate an exhausted, escalated or looping gate framed, whether the frame starts the crate or sits in a joined branch, is reviewed whole at the next gate, and a bare approval of it swaps nothing in. A passed-on frame in a joined branch is unwrapped in place. The reviewer is told a bare approval passes the work on only where it does, never at a self-loop gate. Combined fast/HTTP gates are owned by the coordinator.
Line-job-runner family invariant: I1/I2: the shared hop executor carries the producer verdict and reviewed draft; terse approvals, BLOCKED, and rewritten reviews retain the loop-verdict controls.
