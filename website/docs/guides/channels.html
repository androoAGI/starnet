RUN IT FROM YOUR PHONE

Text your agents from anywhere. They do the work at home.

PLAIN ENGLISH~3 MINNEEDS: A CHANNEL ACCOUNT

01It’s just a chat

Your agents live in your chat app, like any other contact.

Message them the way you’d message a person: give an order, ask what’s going on, change your mind halfway. They answer in the same chat, and the finished work lands there too. Meanwhile the actual work happens on your computer at home — the phone is just how you talk to them.

Works from Telegram, Discord, Slack, Matrix or Signal — whichever you already use.

02Hook one up

  1. Open TOOLSETS

    Find the channel you want and connect it. Each one walks you through its own short setup.

    each channel has its own card
  2. Prove it’s you

    The first message pairs your account as the owner. Only the owner can give the station work — strangers who find the bot get nothing.

    one-time pairing
  3. Say something

    Ask “what are you working on?” or hand over a task. The reply comes back in the chat, from the agent, like a coworker texting you back.

    that’s the whole trick

03Staying in control

Only you

Your account is paired as the owner. Nobody else can command your station through the channel.

Stop a task remotely

Send the stop command from your phone to interrupt the current task in that chat.

Budgets still bind

A job from your phone obeys the same spending caps as one started at the keyboard.

// THE 3AM TESTText an order before bed. Your agents work it overnight. The finished thing is sitting in the chat when you wake up.
