Build your first workflow

A working setup in one click. You just add the agents.

START ●●●~4 MINNEEDS: 1 AGENTBUILDS: A CREWED LINE

You don’t have to build anything to get started.

StarNet comes with four ready-made lines. One click puts a whole working setup on your floor — machines, belts, all connected. Afterwards it’s ordinary machinery: move it, change it, delete half of it.

01Five clicks, start to finish

  1. Open the build screen

    Hit REFIT STATION, then the LINES tool 9. Four ready-made lines appear, each with a little picture.

    four ready-made lines to pick from
  2. Pick one

    Got one agent? Pick SHIP-OUT LOOP. Got two? Pick RESEARCH LINE. You can change anything about it later.

    pick by how many agents you have
  3. Click an empty spot

    The whole line lands at once — machines and belts, already connected. Nothing to wire up.

    everything lands already connected
  4. Give the desks agents

    Each empty desk says NO AGENT — CLICK. So: click it, pick someone. That’s the only thing left to do.

    click each desk, pick someone
  5. Try it

    Close the build screen and hand your station a job. Watch it ride the line and come back finished.

    a real box, a real job
// ROLES ARE SUGGESTIONS, NOT LOCKSEach desk comes with a suggestion — this one wants a RESEARCHER, that one a WRITER. It’s only a suggestion. Put anyone anywhere.

02The four ready-made lines

SHIP-OUT LOOP7 × 2

DESK › OUT TRAY
The smallest one. One agent; everything they finish comes out the tray. Start here if you have one agent.

RESEARCH LINE17 × 2

DOOR › DESK › DESK › OUT TRAY
A job comes in, one agent researches it, the next writes it up, the finished work comes out.

SORTING OFFICE9 × 5

DOOR › SIGNPOST › 2 DESKS
Coding jobs go to one desk, everything else to the other. The sorting is set up for you.

PARALLEL CREW10 × 8

DOOR › SIGNPOST › 3 DESKS
A stream of jobs, three agents, all working at once.

// GIVE IT ROOMA ready-made line won’t drop on top of your furniture. If the click refuses, find a clearer spot or move the furniture first.
