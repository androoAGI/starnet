NIGHT SHIFT

The station works while you’re away — inside a leash you set, with a receipt for every decision.

PLAIN ENGLISH~5 MINNO SETUP NEEDED TO READ

01The whole idea

You go to bed. The station picks a small, grounded job toward your goals, does it, and leaves a draft on your desk.

1

You’ve been away 30 minutes (no clicks, no keys). The station counts you as gone.

2

About every 45 minutes it considers one job. It checks six gates first. Most nights, most beats are skipped.

3

A job that passes runs for real — inside your REACH setting, under your budget caps.

4

You come back to a morning report: what fired, what was skipped and why, drafts to review.

Out of the box the station does nothing unattended. INITIATIVE starts at WAIT. Everything below is you choosing to turn a dial.

02The three-part leash

Open SETTINGS › AUTONOMY. Three rows:

INITIATIVE — does it start work on its own?
WAITnothing runs unless you ask (default)
SUGGESTlines up suggestions you approve — never acts on its own
BUILDdoes a few small grounded jobs a day on its own
FREEpicks & does work toward your goals while you’re away
REACH — how far an unattended action may go
OBSERVEread / research only — writes nothing
SANDBOXbuild & write locally — nothing leaves the machine (default)
SEND & PUBLISHthe highest rung: unattended actions may leave the machine — send, publish, or contact external services
PACE — how many unattended jobs per day
LIGHT 1 · STANDARD 3 (default) · BUSY 6 · MAX 12the daily limit, counted per calendar day
// FREE DOES NOT MEAN UNLEASHEDEven on FREE, the presets keep REACH at SANDBOX. Nothing leaves your machine overnight unless you move REACH to SEND & PUBLISH yourself.

03Point it somewhere

FOCUS

STEER it at a project folder, a thread, or a goal, and every overnight beat starts there. CLEAR hands the choice back to the station.

OFF-LIMITS

RULE OUT anything it should never touch unattended. The station respects the list before it picks a job.

BUDGET

SETTINGS › BUDGET holds hard USD caps — PER RUN, PER AGENT, PER DAY, GLOBAL — enforced against the real ledger. Night Shift can’t spend past them.

04The six gates

Before any beat fires, the station checks, in order: posture (is INITIATIVE above WAIT?), away (have you really been gone 30 minutes?), leash (any jobs left today?), cooldown (45 minutes since the last?), halt (does an older saved automation pause remain?), concurrency (is something already running?). The first gate that says no ends the beat — and that reason is what you read in the morning.

05The morning report

The first time you’re back after a genuine absence, COMMS prints one report. Every line maps to a real record:

  • Priority — what it was working toward, marked (you steered this) if you set a FOCUS, and why.
  • Headline — 3 beats fired, 2 drafts on your desk, or 5 beats skipped — nothing landed on your desk.
  • ✓ one line per draft it produced.
  • Skipped beats, grouped by reason — 2 beats skipped: daily limit reached (by 1:10 AM), in your local time.
  • Drafts in full when you say “show me”.

If nothing fired, it says so plainly and says why. It never pads a quiet night.

06The receipt: RECENT DECISIONS

SETTINGS › NIGHT SHIFT shows the live state — STATE, MODE, DAILY LIMIT, LAST JOB, NEXT JOB EARLIEST — plus RECENT DECISIONS: an append-only trail of every fire, skip, and defer, with the gate that decided it. It is written to disk as it happens and can’t be edited after the fact. ▤ LAST REPORT re-opens the morning report any time.

// PAUSE AWAY-WORKSet INITIATIVE to WAIT to prevent new Night Shift jobs. Use Stop in COMMS for the current task. Older saved global pauses can be cleared with RESUME AUTOMATION, without changing permissions or job settings.

07Questions people actually ask

Can it email people while I sleep?

Only if you set REACH to SEND & PUBLISH and have granted the tool. On OBSERVE or SANDBOX (the default) nothing leaves the machine.

Is there a quiet-hours setting?

No clock. “Away” means no input for 30 minutes, day or night. If you want it to work only while you sleep, leave INITIATIVE at WAIT during the day.

What counts as one of my 3 jobs?

One beat that fired. Skipped beats don’t count. The counter resets each calendar day.

Where do the drafts go?

On your desk in COMMS, listed in the morning report, and under DRAFTS in the NIGHT SHIFT panel until you review them. A gentle nudge reminds you if they sit unreviewed.
