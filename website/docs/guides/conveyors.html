Conveyor basics

A job goes in one end. Finished work comes out the other.

PLAIN ENGLISH~5 MINNO SETUP NEEDED TO READ

01The whole idea

You hand your station a job: “put together a report on my competitors.”

1

The job shows up as a box on the belt.

2

It reaches the first agent. Their part: dig up the data.

3

What they produced rides on to the next agent. Their part: turn it into the report.

4

The finished report lands in the out tray, ready to use.

That’s all a belt is: an assembly line for work. Each agent owns one part of the job and passes their output down the line. And the box is not a cartoon — when you see it move, an agent is really working, right then. Still floor = nothing running.

A BOXthe job, in transit
A DESKone agent, one part of the job
A BELTthe path the work takes
A LINEthe whole job, start to finish

02Why bother?

Each agent owns one part

One agent doing the whole job does every part half-well. On a line, the researcher researches, the writer writes, the checker checks.

The job runs without you

Hand work over at 3am, or have it arrive on a schedule. It moves through the line on its own. You come back to the finished thing.

You can watch the work

No spinner. No hidden queue. If your job is being worked on, you can see its box moving down the line.

03The four pieces

Open REFIT STATION to build. Everything below is under PROP 6, and the belt itself is BELT 7.

THE FRONT DOOR
called an INBOX
Work from outside comes in here. A task you send in. A job on a schedule.
A DESK
called a BAY
One agent sits here. When a box reaches their desk, they do their part of the job.
THE OUT TRAY
called an OUTBOX
Finished work comes out here. Click it to collect it.
A SIGNPOST
FILTER · SPLITTER · JOINER · LOOP
Sits on the belt. Points boxes left or right.
// YOU CAN SKIP THE FRONT DOORChatting with an agent needs no belts at all. Belts are for real jobs — work with parts, work that should flow through several agents on its own.

04Build one

  1. Put down three things

    Open REFIT STATION. Take the PROP tool 6. Place a front door, a desk, and an out tray in a row. Leave a small gap between each.

    machines down, gaps left for belt
  2. Draw the belt

    Take the BELT tool 7. Click the front door. Then click the desk. A belt appears between them. Do the same from the desk to the out tray.

    BELT tool: two clicks per link
  3. Give the desk an agent

    Click the desk and pick someone. Watch the belt: it lights up. Lit means “this will work.”

    a bound bay energizes the run
  4. Set a spending cap

    Click the front door. Give the line a name and a budget. If you skip this, it’s $2.00 per job, and that’s the most it can spend.

    the INBOX card holds the caps
  5. Try it

    Close the build screen. Send your station a task. Watch its box come out of the front door, slide to the desk, and drop in. Your agent is on it right now.

    a real crate = a real run
// OR SKIP ALL FIVE STEPSThe LINES tool 9 drops a whole finished line in one click. See Your First Working Line.

05Is it working?

Lit belt = will work. Dark belt = something’s missing.

You don’t have to test anything. Every time you change a belt, the app checks it and shows the result on the floor right away.

GLOWING

This belt ends at a desk with an agent. It moves. Boxes will ride it.

DARK

Same belt — but the desk is empty. Nothing moves until you fix that.

// THE ONE EVERYONE HITSA belt that just touches the corner of a signpost does nothing. The belt must go through the signpost’s square: in one side, out the other.

06Four shapes cover everything

Same job, four ways to run it through your agents. Each shape is just belts plus one signpost.

ONE AFTER ANOTHERHAND-OFF

An assembly line. Each agent does their part and passes their output to the next. What comes out is the last agent’s finished work.

THIS WAY OR THAT WAYFILTER

The signpost reads each job and routes it to the right specialist. Code goes to the engineer. Everything else goes to the generalist.

ALL AT ONCESPLITTER

A stream of jobs, three agents. The signpost deals them out so all three work at the same time. Nothing gets lost.

ROUND AGAINLOOP

Draft, critique, redraft. The work goes round up to 5 times, then comes out. It can never loop forever or overspend.

// THE EXPENSIVE MISTAKEDesk A sends to desk B, and desk B sends back to desk A? That would run forever and bill you forever. The app refuses to run it, and tells you which belt to cut.
// A BROKEN STEP NEVER EATS YOUR WORKIf a step fails or the budget runs out mid-line, you still get everything finished so far, plus one line telling you where it stopped.

07Something’s not running

When a line won’t run, the reason is written on the machine itself — before anything costs you money. It’s almost always one of these four:

NO AGENT — CLICK

This desk is empty. Click it and pick an agent.

NOT ON THE LINE

No belt touches this desk. Connect it with BELT 7.

NOT FED

The belts don’t actually reach this desk. Usually one is clipping the corner of a signpost instead of going through it.

NOT CONNECTED

The front door has no belt out. Click the door, then click a desk.

The other 8 messages, and what each one means
DUP AGENT — ONE BAY EACH

The same agent is at two desks. One desk each.

A PROP SITS ON THIS LINE — MOVE IT

Furniture is sitting on a belt square. Move the furniture.

SPLITTER NEEDS 2 OUT-LANES

A splitter needs two ways out. Run belts through it: in one side, out two.

JOINER NEEDS 2 IN-LANES

A joiner waits for several belts to arrive. It needs at least two coming in.

NO DEFAULT LANE — CLICK

The filter doesn’t know where “everything else” goes. Click it and pick a lane.

LOOP! — BREAK THE CIRCLE

Your belts make a ring. Boxes would circle forever. Delete one square, or add a LOOP tile.

WORK LINE LOOPS — CUT ONE HANDOFF

Two desks feed each other. That runs forever and bills forever. Cut one belt.

LOOP HAS NO DONE / BACK LANE

A LOOP tile needs two exits: one going onward, one going back.

// NOTHING YOU DO HERE IS PERMANENTDELETE 5 removes anything, and UNDO brings it back. You cannot break your station by experimenting.

08Questions people actually ask

Do I even need belts?

No. You can just chat with an agent, no belts anywhere. Belts are for jobs that should flow through several agents on their own — especially ones that arrive while you’re away.

My belt is dark. Why?

It doesn’t end anywhere useful yet. Follow it to the end. You’ll find an empty desk, a gap, or a belt clipping the corner of a signpost.

Can two belts go to the same desk?

Yes. Boxes line up and wait their turn. The only thing you can’t do is put one agent at two desks.

What if it gets expensive?

Every line has a budget — $2.00 per job unless you raise it. At the cap it stops, hands over what it finished, and says so.

Does it stop when I close the window?

No. The work keeps going. The moving box is just a picture of it — the picture catches up next time you look.
