The new wide service panel is intentionally shallow; do not stretch it to the legacy near-square visual height. The workbench includes a small 1px worktop overhang on either side of its 24px collision width. Its pegboard must not be stretched vertically to the old 27px silhouette. All assets are authored south-facing only; do not rotate upright image pixels by 90 degrees to simulate a side view.

## Export and alpha verification

The generator returned opaque RGB checkerboard mattes for all four sources despite transparent output requests. A targeted background-only correction returned the same issue and subtly changed the cabinet body texture, so it was rejected. The selected originals were packaged by dev/industrial-textures/prepare-support-props.cjs: only connected bright-neutral background plus explicitly inspected enclosed air gaps were removed; no subject pixels were recolored, resized, repainted, sharpened or gamma-adjusted. Output PNGs have real alpha and were inspected against a dark backdrop. Original generated files remain preserved.

Native @napi-rs/canvas decoding passed for all four PNGs. Every retained opaque RGB pixel was compared to its original generated source and matched exactly (3968627 pixels). Screen polygons are normalized against the trimmed sprite bounds and target inset dark glass, excluding frame hardware. All screens are off in the art. support-metadata.json records source dimensions, crop, anchors, visible ground-contact bounds, screen polygons, hashes and export receipts. A wall-mounted panel uses the bottom attachment baseline rather than actual floor-contact feet.
