# Seventeen painted storage and publication props

This set responds to the user's correction that batch02 leaned too hyperrealistic. Twelve storage designs retain their identities, silhouettes and material variety while replacing realistic grain/fibers with broad painted planes and controlled dark edges. The wooden bookshelf calibrated the working balance against the owner's workstation; the remaining eleven were individually restyled using their original image plus those style references. This working balance is not a claim of user approval.

Five additional full new operational designs expand coverage: outbox, connector_portal, pub_publishpress, pub_outboundchute and pub_mailpod. They were generated from workstation/crate/painted-bookshelf references without old sprite tracing. Cargo regions are empty and indicators are dark. The publish press carries raw paper stock but no printed results. Connector sockets are unbound. No output count, completed crate, fake connection state or live work was authored into these images.

Finals: frontend/assets/industrial/batch03/storage/{id}.png. Each intact generated source is sources/{id}.png; exact prompts and roles are {id}.prompt.json; verified alpha/hash/source dimensions/crop/native bounds/anchor/lighting/motion contracts are {id}.export.json. The sibling asset manifest is an export ledger, not a runtime renderer manifest. export-storage.cjs performs user-authorized connected magenta masking and crop only; retained RGB never changes. Every export has nonempty opaque and transparent pixels, zero partial alpha and unchanged source hashes.

Inspected: every full generated source, every 4x world-envelope preview, the 12-prop storage-contact-sheet.png including 1x views, and the five-prop publication-contact-sheet.png. Uniform fitting preserves authored proportions instead of stretching skinny shapes. The bookshelf remains 2x1 tiles with (-1,-9,26,22) envelope and standing south approach. All other original footprints and mount contracts are recorded per asset.

publication-anchors.json and per-ID anchors.json record conservative manually selected source regions for cargo, statuses and display areas, converted through the same uniform bottom-centered fit into world pixels (12 pixels per tile). They are measured proposals requiring live review, not runtime-verified coordinates. The outbox existing shipped-pallet origin at footprint center/bottom+6 is explicitly retained. The press platen is parked in authored art: preserving a moving platen requires masking and recomposition before animation. Native connector truth, route direction, real counts, flashes, motion and lighting remain owner responsibilities.

No runtime, shared manifest, original owned sprites or other worktrees changed. No live integration, floor contact beside agents, behavior acceptance or test:fast gate claimed. Parent/owner owns those checks. Reserved equipment IDs were not generated.
