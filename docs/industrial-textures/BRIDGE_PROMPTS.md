The two user-supplied images are the sole style references. Final assets are packaged in `frontend/assets/industrial/`; `dev/industrial-textures/prepare-bridge.cjs` records their master filenames and preserves generated alpha. The first perimeter master is superseded by the transparency pass.

Complete object centered with generous transparent margin. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT PNG alpha background, not painted checkerboard. Keep the cyan faint precise screen lines with dark black glass, no giant bright blue surface.
